How an arb is born

An arb lives for a few seconds. The scanner finds it.

  1. STEP 1 / 4

    A match is on

    Liverpool vs Chelsea, live. “Total 3.5” market: two outcomes, over or under 3.5 goals. After every chance bookmakers recalculate their odds.

  2. STEP 2 / 4

    One bookmaker lagged behind

    79th minute, 1:2. Time is running out, and bookmakers cut the odds on “under 3.5” to 2.25. superbet.ro still holds 2.38. On “over 3.5” 888sport.com offers 1.87.

  3. STEP 3 / 4

    The scanner sees the arb

    The scanner splits $1 000: $560 on “over” and $440 on “under”. Whatever the outcome, $1 047 comes back, more than you staked. So there is an arb: +$47 (+4.7%).

  4. STEP 4 / 4

    Place it in time

    The calculator helps you stay in profit. The GO button opens the bet slips before superbet.ro fixes its price.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is it legal?

The service only compares public odds and shows discrepancies. You place the bets yourself, at bookmakers where you have an account.

No law forbids betting on the same match at different bookmakers. But bookmakers’ own rules are often against this kind of play, and gambling laws depend on your country. Bet with licensed bookmakers.

Do bookmakers ban you for arbing?

Yes, it’s a real risk. Bookmakers notice systematic arbing and cut limits: the maximum stake drops to a token amount. Sometimes they restrict the account completely.

The service doesn’t bet for you or manage your accounts. It shows arbs and calculates stakes; the decision and the responsibility are yours.

Do I need experience?

No manual maths: the calculator shows how much to stake on each outcome right away. But it’s worth understanding the basics.

A beginner’s main risk: while you place the second leg, the odds change and the arb disappears. Then one bet is left unhedged. This happens more often in live, so start with small amounts.

How much can I make?

It depends on your arb stake, how many bookmakers you use and your limits there. Live arbs in the scanner usually return 2–8% of the arb stake: with a $200 stake that is $4 to $16 per arb.

Your monthly result is the sum of the arbs you managed to place. Cut limits and legs you didn’t place in time reduce it. Income is not guaranteed, so start with small amounts.

How do I pay?

In USDT. Two ways: an invoice in the @CryptoBot Telegram bot, two taps if you have a wallet there. Or a transfer from any wallet or exchange to the address on the invoice.

The subscription doesn’t renew automatically: you pay for the term upfront, and the bot reminds you before it ends.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. On two days a week the scanner is open for free for 1 hour: the feed, the calculator and the GO button work in full. You only need to sign up. The free-hour schedule is in your account, and the bot reminds you 15 minutes before it starts.

The free hour is tied to your account and one device. If an hour isn’t enough, there is a 1-day plan for $15.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Unused full days can be moved to your account balance at any time. You can withdraw money to a wallet within 14 days of payment, minus the network fee. The 1-day plan is non-refundable.

Refunds go to the same wallet and network the payment came from. Details in the payment and refund policy (in Russian).

How do I keep my bookmaker limits longer?

There are no guarantees, but some habits help: round stakes to whole numbers (the calculator does this by default), don’t bet the maximum straight away, mix arbs with regular bets and don’t keep all your money at one bookmaker.

The scanner doesn’t bet for you and doesn’t log in to your accounts. How much to bet and where is up to you.

Do I have to give the service my bookmaker logins?

No. You log in to bookmakers yourself, in your own browser. The extension only opens the right bet slip in the bookmaker’s tab. You enter and confirm the stake yourself.

The scanner doesn’t store bookmaker passwords and can’t see your balances.
