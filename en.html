How an arb is born

Two bookmakers got it wrong in different ways. You take the difference.

  1. STEP 1 / 4

    A match is on

    Live tennis, “Match winner” market. Two outcomes, no draw. The odds change after every point.

  2. STEP 2 / 4

    The lines diverge

    bet365.com offers 2.10 on player one, 888sport.com offers 2.05 on player two. Each looks ordinary on its own.

  3. STEP 3 / 4

    The scanner spots the gap

    Sum of the inverse odds: 1/2.10 + 1/2.05 = 0.9640. That’s less than 1, so there’s an arb: 3.73%.

  4. STEP 4 / 4

    Two bets lock in a profit

    $1 000 is split proportionally. Whoever wins, the payout is the same: +$37.35.

    FAQ

    Straight answers

    Is it legal?

    The service only compares public odds and shows discrepancies. You place the bets yourself, at bookmakers where you have an account.

    No law forbids betting on the same match at different bookmakers. But bookmakers’ own rules are often against this kind of play, and gambling laws depend on your country. Bet with licensed bookmakers.

    Do bookmakers ban you for arbing?

    Yes, it’s a real risk. Bookmakers notice systematic arbing and cut limits: the maximum stake drops to a token amount. Sometimes they restrict the account completely.

    The service doesn’t bet for you or manage your accounts. It shows arbs and calculates stakes; the decision and the responsibility are yours.

    Do I need experience?

    No manual maths: the calculator shows how much to stake on each outcome right away. But it’s worth understanding the basics.

    A beginner’s main risk: while you place the second leg, the odds change and the arb disappears. Then one bet is left unhedged. This happens more often in live, so start with small amounts.

    How is an arb different from a value bet?

    Arb: bets on every outcome, the match result doesn’t matter. The return is small but predictable.

    Value: a single bet at odds above the fair price. Any single bet can lose; the edge only shows over the long run.

    The subscription doesn’t renew automatically: you pay for the term upfront, and the bot reminds you before it ends.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes. Unused full days can be moved to your account balance at any time. You can withdraw money to a wallet within 14 days of payment, minus the network fee. The 1-day plan is non-refundable.

    Refunds go to the same wallet and network the payment came from. Details in the payment and refund policy (in Russian).

    How do I keep my bookmaker limits longer?

    There are no guarantees, but some habits help: round stakes to whole numbers (the calculator does this by default), don’t bet the maximum straight away, mix arbs with regular bets and don’t keep all your money at one bookmaker.

    The scanner doesn’t bet for you and doesn’t log in to your accounts. How much to bet and where is up to you.

    Do I have to give the service my bookmaker logins?

    No. You log in to bookmakers yourself, in your own browser. The GO extension only opens the right bet slip in the bookmaker’s tab. You enter and confirm the stake yourself.

    The scanner doesn’t store bookmaker passwords and can’t see your balances.
