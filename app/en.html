Subscription and billing

Pay in crypto. The subscription is extended by the term you pay for, nothing is charged without your consent

How payment works

  1. Choose a term: 1 day, 1 week or 1 month. The invoice is in USDT
  2. Pay via @CryptoBot in Telegram or by transfer from any wallet or exchange
  3. The network confirms the transfer, usually within 1–5 minutes. The subscription turns on automatically and the scanner opens with no time limit
The bot reminds you before the subscription ends: 3 days and 1 day before for a week or a month, 3 hours before for the 1-day plan. Weekly and monthly plans keep access for 48 more hours; the 1-day plan has no grace period

GO extension

The GO button in the scanner instantly opens the bet slip at the right bookmaker

Getting started

  1. Download the extension here: it is available right after you sign up
  2. Log in to your bookmaker accounts in a separate browser
  3. Install the extension in that browser
  4. Hit GO on each leg of the arb and the bet slip opens automatically
